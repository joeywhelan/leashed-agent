Colours: Elastic teal (#00BFB3) for Elastic components (the CLI, the index), purple (#8C5BFF) for the agent's artefacts, orange (#F5A700) for the human's context, light blue (#1BA9F5) for flow arrows and the "before" counts, pink (#F04E98) only for the refusal receipt, yellow (#FEC514) only for the two zeros. **Design principle:**
The image communicates a single idea: "the agent did the reading and the reasoning, the human checked the receipts and turned the keys, and the two mechanical defects are gone." The two zeros must be the only yellow in the frame, and the refusal receipt the only pink.

**Text accuracy:**
Every string must be copied character-for-character; the only permitted strings in the frame are: `out/report.md`, `out/transcript.jsonl`, `command_blocked`, `cloud api key: 0`, `project password: 0`, `agent api key: 0`, `--use-context admin`, `update-by-query: category`, `update-by-query: name`, `150 → 0`, `60 → 0`, `products`. Watch for doubled consonants ("Serrverless"-type errors) and proofread every label letter by letter before delivering; regenerate rather than deliver a misspelled frame.

**Exclusions:** No legend, caption box, title, footnote, colour key, or any explanatory text block. No logos, brand marks, icons of real products, or watermarks. No depiction of a person or a hand. Only the nodes, panels, arrows, and labels listed above appear in the frame.
